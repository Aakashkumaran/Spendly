How it works
Everything you need to know
The score formula
Your score (0–100) reflects how disciplined your spending is, relative to your income. It's a 7-day weighted average — recent days matter more than older ones.
weighted_spend = Σ(amount × category_weight)
daily_score = 100 − clamp(weighted_spend/income × 120, 0, 100)
final = (prev_6d_avg × 0.6) + (today × 0.4)
Category weights
Fixed costs barely move your score. Impulse buys hit hard. This mirrors how spending actually affects your finances.

Anti-cheat rules
🔐
Income locks for 30 days. You set it once, it sticks. Daily limit can be changed every 15 days.
📅
Score has inertia. One day of zero spends can't save a bad month. One splurge can't ruin a good week.
🙋
You can only cheat yourself. The score is yours. Log honestly or it means nothing.
Streaks & reports
🔥
A streak counts every day your score stays at 65+. Loss aversion is powerful — you won't skip a log to break one.
📋
Weekly reports auto-appear Sunday at noon. Monthly report appears in the last 3 days of the month. Both include a narrative + day-by-day breakdown.
